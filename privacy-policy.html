Maritime Calculator

Privacy Policy

Applies to version 1.6.2 and later · Last updated 10 November 2026

Maritime Calculator is developed and published by Win Tayzar Mein (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what the app does with your information, for the Android, iOS, Windows and macOS builds.

In short

  • Your calculations, vessel data and imported files stay on your device.
  • There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • The app uses the internet only to check for updates.
  • We do not collect, sell or share your personal data.
  • Location is used only when you ask for it, and is never sent to us.

1. Information the app uses on your device

Location (optional, on request only)

  • The app requests location access only when you tap a “use current location” button — on the Star Finder, Compass Error and Magnetic Course screens.
  • With your permission, the app reads your device’s current position once and converts it into latitude and longitude values in the on-screen form.
  • Location is never accessed in the background, never tracked over time, and never sent to us.
  • If you save a calculation, the coordinates visible in that form are stored with the saved entry on your device.
  • You may refuse or revoke permission at any time. The app stays fully usable — you simply enter coordinates manually.

Information you type into the app

Vessel particulars, drafts, hydrostatic tables, survey readings, times, coordinates and other calculation inputs are processed on your device. They are saved only if you choose to save them, and only in the app’s private storage. They are not uploaded to us.

Files you import or export

The Draft Survey module can import ship data from and export results to .xlsx files. Files are accessed only through the system file picker, and only for the files you select. Their contents are not uploaded anywhere.

Clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling the app deletes this local data.

2. Network connections

The app uses the internet only to check for updates. It does not upload your calculation inputs, vessel data, imported files or GPS location to us.

Update checks (GitHub)

  • At most once every 24 hours after launch, and whenever you tap Check for updates in the About screen, the app requests a small public JSON file from GitHub at github.com/tayzarmein/maritime-calculator-releases.
  • The request contains no calculation data or account information. GitHub receives standard technical request data such as your IP address, the request time and the app’s user agent, as it would for any HTTPS request.
  • The response is used only to compare version numbers and, if a newer version exists, to show an update notification. It is not used to track you.
  • GitHub processes that request under its own privacy statement: docs.github.com → GitHub Privacy Statement.

Advertising

This version of the app does not display advertising. If a future version introduces advertising, this policy will be updated and the change described in the app and on the store listing before that version is released.

3. Information we do not collect

We do not collect or receive:

  • your name, email address, phone number or contacts, unless you contact us;
  • your GPS location or any location history;
  • your calculation inputs, vessel particulars, hydrostatic tables or imported files;
  • account credentials, because the app has no accounts;
  • analytics or crash reports, because the app does not use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

The app does not use advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. It does not upload your calculations, vessel data, imported files or GPS location to us.

4. Third-party services

  • GitHub — serves the public update manifest. Receives standard request metadata such as your IP address.
  • Google Play — distributes the Android app and may collect its own install, crash and performance statistics. That collection is governed by Google’s privacy policy, and we may see only aggregated reports.
  • Support email and Telegram — open only when you tap them. If you contact us, we receive only what you choose to send, and we use it solely to reply. We do not add you to mailing lists or sell that correspondence.
  • Device backups — if you enable Android backup, the operating system may include app data in the backup stored in your Google account. We have no access to those backups. You can manage or delete them in your Google account settings.

5. Sharing and selling of data

We do not sell, rent, trade or otherwise share personal data. We do not receive your calculation data, so there is nothing for us to share.

We may disclose support correspondence if required by law or to protect our legal rights.

6. Security

Your local data stays inside the app’s private storage, protected by your device’s operating system and screen lock. Network requests use HTTPS. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we recommend keeping your device updated and locked.

7. Your choices and control

  • Location: revoke at any time in Settings → Apps → Maritime Calculator → Permissions.
  • Saved calculations: delete them in the app, clear all app data in system settings, or uninstall the app.
  • Backups: manage or delete backed-up app data in your Google account settings.
  • Correspondence: email us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of anything you have sent us.

8. Children’s privacy

The app is a professional tool intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has used the app or sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your rights (EEA, UK, California and elsewhere)

Because the app processes your calculation data only on your own device, we do not hold that data and cannot access it.

If you contact us, we process your contact details and message to answer you. We keep that correspondence only as long as needed for that purpose and do not share it. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to that processing, or to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on your request or our legitimate interests for support correspondence.

California residents: we do not sell personal information.

10. Data retention

  • On-device data stays until you delete it, clear the app’s storage or uninstall the app.
  • Support correspondence is kept only as long as needed to answer you and handle any follow-up.
  • Update-check requests are not stored by us; GitHub handles them under its own policies.

11. International transfers

GitHub and Google Play may process data in the United States and other countries. They use the safeguards described in their own privacy policies for those transfers. We do not operate servers of our own.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app’s behaviour changes. The “last updated” date at the top will change, and material changes will be announced in the app or on the store listing. Continuing to use the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
