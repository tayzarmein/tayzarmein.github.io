Maritime Calculator

Privacy Policy

Applies to version 1.4.0 and later · Last updated 29 October 2026

Maritime Calculator is developed and published by Win Tayzar Mein (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what the app does with your information, for the Android, iOS, Windows and macOS builds.

In short

  • The app runs entirely on your device.
  • There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • We do not collect, transmit, sell or share your personal data.
  • Location is used only when you ask for it, and never leaves your device.

1. Information the app uses on your device

Location (optional, on request only)

  • The app requests location access only when you tap a “use current location” button — on the Star Finder, Compass Error and Magnetic Course screens.
  • With your permission, the app reads your device’s current position once and converts it into latitude and longitude values in the on-screen form.
  • Location is never accessed in the background, never tracked over time, and never sent off your device.
  • If you save a calculation, the coordinates visible in that form are stored with the saved entry on your device.
  • You may refuse or revoke permission at any time. The app stays fully usable — you simply enter coordinates manually.

Information you type into the app

Vessel particulars, drafts, hydrostatic tables, survey readings, times, coordinates and other calculation inputs are processed on your device. They are saved only if you choose to save them, and only in the app’s private storage.

Files you import or export

The Draft Survey module can import ship data from and export results to .xlsx files. Files are accessed only through the system file picker, and only for the files you select. Their contents are not uploaded anywhere.

Clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling the app deletes this local data.

2. Information we do not collect

The app does not collect or transmit:

  • your name, email address, phone number or contacts;
  • your location or any location history;
  • device identifiers, advertising ID, usage statistics or diagnostics;
  • crash reports or analytics;
  • any other personal data.

The Android build is compiled without the INTERNET permission, so it is technically unable to transmit data from your device. The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or social-media SDKs.

3. Third-party services

The app does not use any third-party service that receives your data.

Two features open other apps or websites, and only when you tap them:

  • Support email — opens your email app.
  • Telegram support group — opens Telegram or your browser.

Those services operate under their own privacy policies. If you contact us by email or Telegram, we receive only what you choose to send, and we use it solely to reply to you. We do not add you to mailing lists, and we do not sell or share that correspondence.

Google Play may collect its own install, crash and performance statistics as part of distributing the app. That collection is governed by Google’s privacy policy, and we may see only aggregated reports.

Device backups. If you have enabled Android backup on your device, the operating system may include app data in the backup stored in your Google account. We have no access to those backups. You can manage or delete them in your Google account settings.

4. Sharing and selling of data

We do not sell, rent, trade or otherwise share personal data. Because the app does not transmit data, no personal data reaches us or any third party through the app.

5. Security

Your data stays inside the app’s private storage, protected by your device’s operating system and screen lock. Since nothing is transmitted, there is no data in transit to intercept. No method of storage is completely secure, so we recommend keeping your device updated and locked.

6. Your choices and control

  • Location: revoke at any time in Settings → Apps → Maritime Calculator → Permissions.
  • Saved calculations: delete them in the app, clear all app data in system settings, or uninstall the app.
  • Backups: manage or delete backed-up app data in your Google account settings.
  • Correspondence: email us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of anything you have sent us.

7. Children’s privacy

The app is a professional tool intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights (EEA, UK, California and elsewhere)

Because the app processes data only on your own device, we do not hold your app data and cannot access it.

If you contact us, we process your contact details and message for the purpose of answering you. We keep that correspondence only as long as needed for that purpose, and we do not share it. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to that processing, or to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority. To exercise any of these rights, email us. We do not sell personal information.

9. Advertising and purchases

This version of the app contains no advertising and no in-app purchases.

If a future version introduces advertising or in-app purchases, this policy will be updated and the change described in the app and on the store listing before that version is released. Such a version would necessarily require network access and would use advertising or billing services with their own privacy policies.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app’s behaviour changes. The “last updated” date at the top will change, and material changes will be announced in the app or on the store listing. Continuing to use the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
